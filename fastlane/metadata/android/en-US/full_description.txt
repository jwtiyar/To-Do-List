To-Do: Task List & Reminder is an offline task manager and reminder app. It stores your tasks locally on your device without an account or cloud sync.

Features:
- Create, edit, and organize tasks by status and priority
- Set one-time or repeating reminders with exact alarms
- Encrypt and export backups with AES-256
- View pending tasks from your home screen with a resizable widget
- Search tasks by title and notes
- Choose light, dark, or system theme
- Free and open source under GPLv3
